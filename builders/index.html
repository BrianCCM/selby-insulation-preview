Why Builders Work With Selby Insulation

Choosing an insulation subcontractor usually comes down to two questions. Will the crew be there on the day you scheduled, and will the work pass inspection the first time. Our goal is to make that part of your build predictable.

Service within three business days

Most projects are scheduled and completed within three business days of your call. We work from your framing and rough-in dates rather than a general week, and if anything on our end changes we let you know before it reaches your schedule.

One subcontractor for every scope

Fiberglass batts, blown-in attic insulation, open and closed cell spray foam, crawl space work and removal all come from the same crew. That means one bid, one point of contact and one invoice rather than three trades to coordinate.

Installed to pass inspection

We install to the R-values on your energy compliance sheet, set depth markers in the attic and post the certification card where the inspector expects it. Washington and Kane County require R-38 in the ceiling while Iron, Beaver and Garfield County require R-49, and we track which one applies to your job.

Our owner is on every job site

The person who prices your work is the same person supervising the crew installing it. Questions get answered on site instead of going back through a sales representative who is not there.

We price match written estimates

Send us a written bid for the same scope and we will match the price. We would rather earn the job on a number you have already been quoted than lose a repeat builder over it.

A clean handoff to the next trade

We bag scraps, mask overspray and sweep before we leave. Your drywall crew arrives to a space they can start in, which keeps the rest of the schedule where you put it.

Insulation Scopes We Bid

We work on new construction, additions, remodels and tenant improvements, from single family homes through small multifamily projects.

Fiberglass Insulation

R-13 through R-21 in walls and R-30 through R-38 in floors and vaulted assemblies, kraft faced or unfaced. We carry Owens Corning, CertainTeed and Johns Manville and install whichever product your specification calls for. Batts are cut to fit around blocking and wiring rather than compressed past them, which protects the R-value you are paying for.

Blown-In Attic Insulation

The fastest way to reach your ceiling R-value over trusses, with consistent depth across the whole lid. We install baffles at the eaves so the soffit vents keep working and set depth rulers so coverage can be verified at final inspection without a tape measure.

Spray Foam Insulation

Open cell for sound control and interior partitions. Closed cell where the assembly needs air sealing, moisture resistance and high R-value in a shallow cavity. In Southern Utah that usually means vaulted roof decks, rim joists, cathedral ceilings and conditioned attics holding HVAC equipment.

Crawl Space and Subfloor

Batt or foam insulation under the floor, rim joist air sealing, and full encapsulation when the crawl space needs to be brought inside the thermal envelope.

Insulation Removal

Remodels, fire and water damage, and rodent contamination. We vacuum the existing material out, clean the cavity and reinsulate to current code. This comes up regularly on additions where part of the attic is original and part is new.

Home Energy Assessment

A full diagnostic of the finished envelope, including a blower door test, thermal imaging analysis, air leak detection, an HVAC system evaluation and an insulation inspection. Worth doing on custom homes where the owner wants documented performance rather than an assurance.
