What we bid

New construction, additions, remodels and tenant improvements. Single family through small multifamily.

Fiberglass batts

R-13 to R-21 in walls, R-30 and R-38 in floors and vaulted assemblies. Kraft faced or unfaced, Owens Corning, CertainTeed or Johns Manville depending on what your spec calls for. Cut to fit around blocking and wiring rather than stuffed past it.

Blown-in attic insulation

The quick, consistent way to hit R-49 over trusses. Even depth across the whole lid, baffles at the eaves so the soffit vents keep working, and rulers set so depth is easy to verify at final.

Spray foam, open and closed cell

Open cell where you want sound control and a filled cavity. Closed cell where you need air sealing, moisture resistance and real R-value in a shallow bay. Common calls out here are vaulted roof decks, rim joists, cathedral ceilings and sealed attics with ductwork in them.

Crawl space and subfloor

Batts or foam under the floor, rim joist sealing, and encapsulation where the crawl needs to be brought inside the thermal envelope.

Removal and retrofit

Remodels, fire and water loss, rodent contamination. We vacuum the old material out, clean the cavity, and reinsulate to current numbers. Useful on flips and on additions where half the attic is original and half is new.

Energy assessment

A walk of the envelope before or after drywall to find where the plan and the field do not match. Handy on custom homes where the owner is paying attention to the power bill.
